How to Import The
Shared Calendar

Follow the instructions below to subscribe to the shared calendar on your device.

I Own An iPhone I Own An Android

For IOS (Apple)

Step 1:

Copy the .ics link if it hasn't been auto copied to your clipboard.

Step 2:

Open the calendars app on your phone.

Step 3:

Press the calendar button in the bottom right corner.

Step 4:

Press "Add Calendar" (may need to scroll to bottom).

Step 5:

Press "Add Subscription Calendar" (second option).

Step 6:

Paste in the link and click "Find".

Step 7:

Click the red checkmark at the top right of your screen.

Optional:

For event notifications, go to settings → apps → calendar → default notifications → select time.

For Android

Step 1:

Go to the google calendars website via a computer.

Step 2:

Copy the .ics link if it hasn't been auto copied to your clipboard.

Step 3:

In the main menu, scroll down and find "Other Calendars".

Step 4:

Press the plus button and then press "From URL".

Step 5:

Paste the .ics link into the box and press "Add calendar".

Step 6:

Open the Google Calendar app on your mobile phone.

Step 7:

Press the menu button, scroll down to find settings and click it.

Step 8:

Select your account and tap on "Musique Angels" (the calendar).

Step 10:

Make sure the "sync" toggle is set to "on".
